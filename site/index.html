Your bank already tells you everything.

Private. Local. Yours.

SpendWise is an offline personal finance ledger for Android. It reads the transaction alerts your bank already sends you and turns them into a record of your own money, without you typing a single one of them in.

It does not ask Android for the INTERNET permission. SpendWise cannot send your data anywhere. That is not a promise we are making, it is a thing Android will not let the app do.

How it works

You pick which apps SpendWise is allowed to read, one at a time. Your bank, your wallet, your messaging app. When one of them posts an alert about money, SpendWise reads the amount, which way it went and who it was, and files it.

When it is not sure, it asks you instead of guessing. When you correct the same shop a few times, it learns and stops asking.

What it does not have

  • No server, and no network permission to reach one.
  • No account to sign up for, and no profile of you.
  • No analytics, advertising or crash reporting. No third party code of any kind.
  • No bank credentials. It never asks for them, and it could not use them.

Your data

The ledger lives in an encrypted database on your phone. The key is generated on the device and kept in the Android Keystore. Android cloud backup is switched off, so your ledger is not quietly copied into a Google backup either. Export to CSV or JSON whenever you like, and erase everything from inside the app whenever you like.
