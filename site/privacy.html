SpendWise

Privacy policy

Last updated 18 September 2026

SpendWise is an offline personal finance ledger for Android. It reads the notifications your bank already sends you and turns them into a record of your own money.

SpendWise works with no network connection and does not ask Android for the INTERNET permission. It is not capable of sending your data anywhere. Android enforces that, we do not just promise it.

What the app reads, and why

SpendWise asks for notification access, which Android calls BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE. This is what the app is for. A transaction gets recorded only because a notification described it.

  • You choose which apps SpendWise may read, one at a time, in Settings, then Notification sources. Nothing is read from an app you have not enabled.
  • Notifications from the apps you enabled are read on the device, to pull out an amount, a direction, a date, who was paid and a reference number.
  • Notifications that are not about money, such as one time passcodes, delivery updates and marketing, are thrown away rather than stored.
  • SpendWise only reads. It never posts a notification, dismisses one, or acts on one.
  • You can revoke notification access at any time in Android settings, and switch off individual sources inside the app.

SpendWise does not ask for READ_SMS, RECEIVE_SMS or any call log permission. Where your bank sends an SMS, SpendWise sees only the notification your messaging app posts about it, and only if you enabled that app.

What is stored on your device

  • Account names, and which apps are mapped to them
  • The raw notification text, kept as evidence for each entry
  • What the app read out of those alerts, and the transactions it made from them
  • Local preferences, none of them secret

The main database is encrypted with SQLCipher. A random 256 bit key is generated on the device and held in Android secure storage, backed by the Android Keystore. Alerts captured while the app is not running are encrypted field by field with AES-GCM, under a separate key that cannot be exported.

What leaves your device

Nothing. There is no backend, no telemetry, no analytics, no advertising, no remote crash reporting and no AI provider. The app contains no third party code that collects anything.

The one exception is something you do on purpose. An export you start yourself writes a CSV or JSON file to a place you pick, through Android's own document picker. SpendWise does not upload it and cannot see where it went.

Data collection and sharing

SpendWise collects no data, shares no data with anyone, and sells no data.

Deleting your data

In Settings, Erase all local data removes the ledger, the journals, the database key, the capture queue and your source configuration. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored. Since nothing is ever transmitted, there is no copy on a server for you to ask us to delete.

Android cloud backup and device transfer are switched off, using allowBackup="false", so your ledger is not copied into a Google backup.

Children

SpendWise is not aimed at children and does not knowingly process any data from children. There are no accounts, no sign up and no profiles of any kind.

What this does not protect against

Encryption at rest and the Android app sandbox reduce what someone could get from a lost or stolen phone that is locked, or from pulling the storage off it. They do not protect you from malware that already has high privileges on your phone, or from someone using it while it is unlocked.
