Accounts · holdings

Every direction above carries the same five screens in one row, in the same order, so you can read across a row for one world or down a column for one screen.

My read: Confluence (L) is the only one whose main idea is the thing that kept breaking — it shows which account an alert landed in and draws own-account transfers as the one element that crosses lanes. Current (K) is the easier daily read of the two river ideas. Broadsheet (M) is the most distinctive, and the riskiest, because a written lede has to be earned by real rules underneath.
