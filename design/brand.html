Concepts

Shown at 112 px on ground.

01

Split

Income enters as one band and divides. What stays is thick; what leaves is a thread. The home screen, compressed to a glyph.

02

Rule

The ruled ledger. Entry lengths are amounts, bracketed by an opening and a closing rule.

03

Return

One block leaves an account and lands in another, unchanged. The hollow and the solid are the same size: moved, not spent.

04

Ratio

The square is the whole month. The share that leaves is cut on one straight line and set apart.

05

Aperture

Four blades closed on a fixed centre. Privacy as a shutter rather than a shield.

06

Current

Streamlines of one flow, weighted by how much each carries. Reads as a pattern more than a glyph.

07

Meander

An S drawn as a single run of money, stepping down in weight where the spending starts.

02 and 06 are the weak pair. Rule reads as a list icon once it is small, and Current loses its thinnest streamline below 64 px. Both are better used as page furniture than as the app icon.
